# Synapse product review — AKT scope

Updated 1 November 2026 to reflect the owner’s decisions. Synapse supports AKT knowledge learning through questions. OSCEs, observed clinical competence and placement sign-offs are outside this product.

| Perspective | Implemented response | Remaining boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Developer | Owner-only connected service, public local runner, durable answers, conflict protection, backups, synced exam preferences, versioned generation and measurement rules | Physical iOS/Safari and Android checks still matter; emulated phone tests are not device certification. |
| Medical student | Question-led progression, recall before options, optional confidence, personal exam dates, current blocks, homepage activity and automatic weak-point priorities | Missing historic timing/confidence stays unknown. Dates prioritise study but never award mastery. |
| Professor | Original source-supported questions, domain mapping, stage distinction, distractor explanations, fresh retention evidence and audited exclusions | AI source checks and self-review are not independent clinician validation. A clinician review is not a prerequisite for personal use. |
| Observer of study | Evidence-based next actions, transparent counts, errors, retries, trends and retention gaps | A Green topic or fast answer does not predict an exam pass. Use school mocks and official AKT practice to assess readiness. |

Start with foundations on a new topic. Move to focused questions as understanding develops, then clinical application. Review first-answer errors, return for fresh spaced checks and mix topics progressively. The complete original workflow is served by `get_akt_generation_skill`; detailed priorities come from `get_performance_profile`.

The imported UKMLA catalogue, condition/presentation distinction and permanent IDs remain unchanged. The five CCS domains remain intact; stages, difficulty, objectives and measurements are additional dimensions. They are not represented as new official GMC domains.

Ongoing maintenance: keep source references current, exclude faulty items, reconcile catalogue changes through preview, monitor free-tier limits and practise export/restore. No placement-log or OSCE feature is planned under the current scope.
